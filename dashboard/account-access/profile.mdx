# Profile

## What this is for

Your profile is the small card of personal details attached to your account: your name, job title, phone and country. Your name appears in the account menu at the top right of the console. It belongs to you, not to any one organization, so there is one profile no matter how many organizations you join.

## Before you start

- You need to be signed in. Every role can edit their own profile; nobody can edit anyone else's.
- Nothing on this page is required. You can save with empty fields.

## Edit your profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the page">
    Select your initial (the circle at the right of the top bar) to open the account menu, then select **Profile**. The page is titled **Profile** with the line "How you appear to your team."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your email">
    The **Email** box shows the address you sign in with. It is read-only here; you cannot change it from this page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the fields">
    Type into **First name**, **Last name**, **Job title**, **Phone** and **Country**. Use plain text; for example Country can be "Nigeria" or "United States".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Save profile**. The button reads **Saving…** for a moment, then a small message says **Profile saved**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you'll see

A single card with the read-only **Email** box on top, then the editable fields in two columns (one column on a phone), and the **Save profile** button at the bottom. If you have never filled it in, the boxes are simply empty. Clearing a box and saving removes that detail.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Who can see my profile?">
    Your name is shown to you in the account menu. The [Members](/dashboard/organizations/members) list currently shows email addresses rather than names.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change my email address here?">
    No. The email box is read-only on this page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I have to fill in my phone number?">
    No. Every field except the read-only email is optional.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does my profile apply to every organization?">
    Yes. It is attached to your account, so there is one profile no matter how many organizations you join.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Could not save your profile** | The save did not go through, usually a dropped connection or an expired sign-in. Reload the page, sign in again if asked, and retry. |
| The page shows a sign-in screen | Your session ended. Sign in again and reopen **Profile**. |
