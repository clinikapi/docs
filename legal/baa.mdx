# Business Associate Agreement overview

This page is an overview of our Business Associate Agreement. The executed agreement is provided when you sign.

*Last updated: October 24, 2026.*

Bettar Platforms, Inc., through ClinikAPI, provides a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (BAA) for all paid plans (Pro, Team and Enterprise). ClinikAPI is a product of Bettar Platforms, Inc., a Delaware corporation with its registered office at 8 The Green, Suite B, Dover, DE 19901, United States.

## What is a BAA?

A BAA is a legally binding contract required by HIPAA when a covered entity (your organization) shares Protected Health Information (PHI) with a business associate (Bettar Platforms, Inc., through ClinikAPI). It defines:

- How PHI may be used and disclosed
- Safeguards required to protect PHI
- Breach notification procedures
- Termination and data return obligations

## Requesting a BAA

BAAs are available on all paid plans. Current plan amounts are on the [pricing page](https://clinikapi.com/pricing).

| Plan | BAA Available |
|------|---------------|
| Free | No (synthetic data only) |
| Pro | Yes |
| Team | Yes |
| Enterprise | Yes (custom terms) |

To request a BAA:

1. Upgrade to a paid plan in the [Dashboard](https://dashboard.clinikapi.com)
2. Navigate to **Settings > Compliance**
3. Review and sign the BAA electronically

Enterprise customers receive a customized BAA with additional terms.

## Technical Safeguards

Bettar Platforms, Inc., through ClinikAPI, implements the following safeguards as documented in the BAA:

- **Encryption at rest**: AES-256 with cloud-managed KMS keys
- **Encryption in transit**: TLS 1.2+ on all API endpoints
- **Access controls**: API key authentication with tenant isolation
- **Audit logging**: Every API request logged with FHIR resource context
- **Data isolation**: Tenant-tagged resources with enforced `_tag` filtering
- **Breach notification**: Within 15 business days of discovery for the business associate agreement, and within 24 hours for a personal-data breach under the data processing agreement
