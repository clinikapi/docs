1.4 "Personal Data", "Process", "Controller", "Processor", "Sub-processor", "Data Subject", "Supervisory Authority" and "Personal Data Breach" have the meanings given in the Data Protection Laws. Under the NDPA the Supervisory Authority is the NDPC. Article references are to the EU GDPR; where the NDPA or UK GDPR governs, the corresponding provisions of that law apply.

1.5 **Where you are a processor.** Where you are yourself a processor for your own clients (the controllers), you act as a processor and we act as your sub-processor under Article 28(4) of the EU GDPR (and the corresponding provisions of the UK GDPR and the NDPA). ## 3. Processor obligations

3.1 **Documented instructions (Art. 28(3)(a)).** We Process Personal Data only on your documented instructions, including on transfers to a third country, unless Required by Law; in that case we inform you first unless the law prohibits it on important grounds of public interest. This DPA, the Agreement, and your configuration and use of the Services (API calls, dashboard actions) are your complete documented instructions. We tell you without undue delay if, in our opinion, an instruction infringes the Data Protection Laws. We do not Process this Personal Data for our own purposes and do not use it to train, develop or improve any artificial intelligence or machine learning model except as you instruct, and we do not treat information de-identified in accordance with 45 C.F.R. § 164.514 as falling outside this DPA unless that information is anonymous within the meaning of the Data Protection Laws.

3.2 **Confidentiality of personnel (Art. 28(3)(b)).** Each person we authorize to Process Personal Data is bound by an enforceable duty of confidentiality that survives the end of their engagement, has access only as needed to provide the Services or comply with law, and is trained on their obligations.

3.3 **Security (Art. 28(3)(c)).** We implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures for a level of security appropriate to the risk, addressing as appropriate: pseudonymization and encryption; ongoing confidentiality, integrity, availability and resilience; the ability to restore availability and access in a timely manner after an incident; and regular testing and evaluation of the measures. We make a current description available on request and will not materially diminish it during the term (see Annex B).

3.4 **Sub-processors (Art. 28(3)(d), 28(2), 28(4)).** You give general written authorization to engage Sub-processors, subject to this clause.

(a) We maintain a current list of Sub-processors that Process Personal Data on your behalf (each Sub-processor, the Processing it carries out, and its country) and supply it on request to legal@clinikapi.com.

(b) We give at least thirty (30) days' notice before adding or replacing a Sub-processor, by email to the account owner and admins. You may object on reasonable data-protection grounds within that period; if it cannot be resolved in good faith you may terminate the affected Services without penalty before the change takes effect.

(c) We bind each Sub-processor by written contract to data-protection obligations no less protective than this DPA and remain fully liable to you for their performance.

3.5 **Data Subject rights (Art. 28(3)(e)).** Taking into account the nature of the Processing, we assist you by appropriate technical and organizational measures, insofar as possible, in responding to Data Subject requests (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and not being subject to solely automated decisions). The Services let you access, correct and delete data yourself, and we give access to it through the API on any plan, through bulk NDJSON export on Team and Enterprise, and through a one-off export from support on Free and Pro; where you cannot act yourself, we give reasonable assistance. If we receive a request directly from a Data Subject we only acknowledge it, direct them to you, and notify you without undue delay.

3.6 **Breach notification, DPIAs, consultation (Art. 28(3)(f)).**

(a) We notify you of a Personal Data Breach affecting Personal Data we Process for you without undue delay and no later than twenty-four (24) hours after becoming aware of it.

(b) The notice includes what a Controller needs to notify: the nature of the breach, categories and approximate numbers of Data Subjects and records, likely consequences, and measures taken, to the extent known, and is supplemented as information becomes available. We do not delay the first notice to finish investigating.

(c) We give reasonable assistance with security of Processing, notification to Supervisory Authorities and Data Subjects, data protection impact assessments and prior consultation.

(d) We do not notify a Supervisory Authority or Data Subjects, or name you publicly, unless you instruct us or the law requires.

3.7 **Deletion or return (Art. 28(3)(g)).** At your choice, on expiry or termination we delete or return all Personal Data Processed on your behalf and delete existing copies, except as law requires us to retain it. Data on exit. For 30 days after your account is closed or terminated, you can export your data through the API or by asking support to run a bulk export. After that 30-day window your data is permanently deleted, and backup copies age out within 35 days of that deletion. On request, we confirm the deletion in writing. Return is by export during that 30-day window, and deletion follows it. Anything retained because law requires it (for example audit records) is limited to what the requirement demands, stays protected by this DPA, is used only for that purpose and is deleted when the requirement ends. We confirm on request what was deleted, returned or retained.

3.8 **Audits and information (Art. 28(3)(h)).** We keep records of Processing as the Data Protection Laws require and make available the information reasonably necessary to show compliance. We allow and contribute to audits, including inspections, by you or an auditor you mandate: not more than once per 12 months (except after a Personal Data Breach or if a Supervisory Authority requires), on at least 30 days' written notice, in business hours, under confidentiality, and without disrupting the Services or compromising other customers' data. We may satisfy a request with current third-party audit reports, certifications or completed security questionnaires where available and adequate.

3.9 **Automated decision-making.** Where the Services present output of automated Processing, it is for review by your users and is not our decision. We take no decision about a Data Subject producing legal or similarly significant effects on automated Processing alone.
